Cluster disaster recovery adds failover capabilities at the Vault cluster level, complementing existing regional DR. Teams can test recovery procedures, respond to cluster-specific security incidents, and maintain secrets availability during upgrades, available in public preview for HCP Vault Dedicated customers.
